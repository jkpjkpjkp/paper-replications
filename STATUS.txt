Tolerance / uncertainty limits:
Both primary means fall within the corresponding published +/- bands, the
comparison rule frozen before fitting. This supports a scoped approximate
level 2, not rounding agreement or statistical equivalence. Table 1 calls
error bars standard errors, while the current author script prints np.std.
The original error-bar type is unresolved; local SD (ddof=1) and SEM are
reported separately. Split reuse also prevents treating the SEM as independent
population-level uncertainty. If the original bars are SD, a precision-based
comparison of means would be stricter than this descriptive band criterion.

PRESERVED FAILED DIAGNOSTIC / PROTOCOL DISCREPANCY:

validation log-likelihood. The predeclared MSE-selection diagnostic on the same

absolute gap 1.751416781 (875.708391% arithmetic relative error), failing the
0.26 tolerance. Its RMSE is 0.611005447 +/- 0.225330579 split SD. Nine diagnostic
selections hit the cap. This discrepancy is not resolved by the primary result.
It is not an exact replay of the original code/runtime or proof of paper error.
