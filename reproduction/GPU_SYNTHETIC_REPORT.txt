Metric interpretation and important limitation
The comparison above uses the release's final pooled-sample evaluation
(nets_toy.eval_diffusion), not its per-mini-batch training-monitor metric.
The latter prints final test loss .4356 and uses different sampled decisions.
All saved final decisions satisfy the [0,2] constraints with zero violation.
Independent empirical test-loss recomputation agrees within 5e-9.

Under the known released data-generating law, exact population expected cost
of the saved TEST decisions is 0.481246088 for baseline and
0.519134453 for DFL (lower is better). Thus this seed's empirical
test improvement does NOT establish a population improvement. Hold-out pooled
cost is also worse for DFL: 2.871287 versus 1.382123.

The paper specifies p=.8 and no .5 scaling, with no context; those settings
conflict with released defaults. The separate --protocol paper mode is
implemented but was NOT executed in this session. The population optimum for
the stated paper law is .9293755313, versus .4005598628 for the released law;
see WRONG.txt for evidence and the distinction between finite empirical losses
and population expectations. More compute alone cannot reconcile specification
ambiguity. Required next work: paper-law run and multiple independent seeds,
with consistent evaluation and predeclared tolerances.
