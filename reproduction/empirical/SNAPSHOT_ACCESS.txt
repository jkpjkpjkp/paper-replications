HOW TO OBTAIN HISTORICAL SNAPSHOT DATA — checked 2026-11-18

1. Ask your institution's WRDS/library data administrator whether its S&P
   subscription includes Compustat Snapshot, product/schema comp_snapshot.
   Standard Compustat North America and compsamp_snapshot sample access do not
   establish this entitlement. If licensed, ask WRDS to check your account access.

2. If the institution does not license it, ask the administrator or S&P Global
   Market Intelligence for an institutional subscription/quote. WRDS's contact
   page explicitly supports adding data to an existing account. Do not assume
   that a new token or downloading the standard data again will add entitlement.

Request text (draft only; not sent):
"We are reproducing Chauhan et al. (ICML 2020), Uncertainty-Aware Lookahead
Factor Models for Quantitative Investing. Our WRDS account can query standard
Compustat, but SELECT on comp_snapshot.wrds_csa_pit and
comp_snapshot.wrds_csq_pit returns SQLSTATE 42501, permission denied for schema
comp_snapshot. Does our institution license Compustat Snapshot? If so, please
enable/check our entitlement. Otherwise, please advise on adding the product.
We need annual and quarterly historical versions, effective/availability dates,
active and inactive US firms, and clarification of vintage coverage for a study
spanning 1970–2019. We need the full product, not sample tables."

3. A second route is to ask the paper's authors for their processed monthly panel,
   exact field mapping and vintage/availability construction, subject to their
   redistribution license. They may be able to provide preprocessing code even
   if the proprietary data cannot be shared. No request has been sent.

Coverage caution: S&P's current public Compustat Financials page advertises
point-in-time snapshots from 1987. The paper combines North America and Snapshot
over 1970–2019, but does not fully specify its pre-Snapshot construction. Ask the
vendor/authors about actual vintage start dates; old fiscal dates are not proof
of archived information availability on those dates. comp_pit is a distinct
product, not automatically an interchangeable replacement.
