WORD-FOR-WORD CLAIM LIMITS / UNSPECIFIED CHOICES
1. The paper states five different seeds but does not name their values.
   Predeclaring five new distinct seeds would implement the stated independent-
   run design; it would not recover the authors' exact original random streams.
2. The paper says best validation performance without naming its metric,
   direction, tie handling, or whether selection is across seed-averaged
   validation scores or independently per seed. The released training uses
   validation CVaR for checkpoint selection; that does not establish the
   authors' original hyperparameter-selection rule.
3. The release halves the learning rate with lradj=type1. Appendix D states
   learning rate 0.001 but does not specify a schedule or explicitly say it
   remains constant. Neither interpretation can be labeled uniquely exact.
4. The exact runtime in requirements.txt is
   torch==2.6.0a0+ecf3bae40a.nv25.1. The completed runs used 2.4.1+cu121.
   The exact author GPU, precision flags, checkpoint and RNG states are absent.
5. Other implementation details supplied by public code must retain that
   provenance; they must not be described as explicit paper wording.
