WHY THE ORIGINAL HEADLINE DOES NOT FOLLOW
1. Profit is an estimate, significance is evidence, alpha is factor-model-relative,
   and paired improvement over a baseline is a separate estimand.
2. A p-value above .05 is not proof of zero expected performance. The after-cost
   alpha interval still permits an economically material positive effect.
3. A negative point estimate alone is not evidence of baseline inferiority.
4. A constant forecast requires a tie rule before it defines a decile strategy.
   A coherent tie-cancelling/no-trade control has zero spread and undefined Sharpe,
   but no matched CZ202 predictive-baseline ledger is supplied in the completed run.
5. The directory contains significant positive gross-return evidence in other
   LiMT designs. These are outside the current protocol, and must be disclosed.
6. The legacy programme has 437 catalogue records, not 437 completed compatible
   reproductions. Its return extension records 273 pending, 52 blocked,
   110 not applicable, and two completed positive-inconclusive records.
7. Financial QA, credit risk, fraud detection and similar tasks need their own
   objectives; a stock-profitability screen cannot establish their irrelevance.

NEW ROBUSTNESS CALCULATIONS
Circular paired (return,factors) block bootstrap, 2,000 resamples per cost/block
case, NumPy random seed 20261110, percentile intervals for annual OLS alpha.
Blocks20/60 give gross intervals [-2.24,12.96]% / [-2.62,12.86]% and cost10
intervals [-9.73,6.02]% / [-9.40,6.21]%. These are post hoc robustness diagnostics,
not preregistered tests. They do not adjust for the historical research search.

BEFORE THIS CAN SUPPORT A STRONG IJCAI EMPIRICAL SUBMISSION
Priority 1: Complete matched linear/ridge, tree, simple signal and no-information
controls on identical dates and eligibility, with the same exposure and fee rules.
Test paired return and alpha increments. Preserve all alternatives, including wins.
Priority 2: Complete further selected RETURN-PREDICTION models under the actual
protocol. The inspected CS-GAT protocol202 directory has prepared data but no
completed return inference in the audit snapshot; preparation is not a null result.
Use a documented inclusion rule to avoid selecting models for their failures.
Priority 3: Extend beyond the historical large-500 universe. Report whether all
202 predictors remain available/varying, source-month availability and missingness.
Priority 4: Use a genuinely untouched or prospective confirmation period and a
predeclared economically meaningful margin. Do not label already-inspected
historical data a new holdout. Freeze the complete model/portfolio/cost search.
Priority 5: Retain original-task reproductions separately. Replacing native daily
market inputs with monthly CZ descriptors can change the task and handicap a
temporal model; a failure of transfer does not refute the published benchmark.
Priority 6: Human authors must check the exact source support and bibliographic
metadata, approve scientific claims and authorship, finalize declarations and
release permissions, and decide submission. The draft remains submission_ready=false.
These are research limitations, not an outstanding request to approve an action.
