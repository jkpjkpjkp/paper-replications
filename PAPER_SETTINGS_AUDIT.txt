What the paper says about variability
Section 4.1, page 8, explicitly reports a fixed global seed. Its numeric value
is not provided. Table 3 reports single ASR values, without seed SD/SEM or a
multi-seed Sharpe distribution. The public replies do not add such a study.

Related issues were discussed. Reviewer zHmG asked about sensitivity to path
count and crisis regimes. Reviewer b8bB requested an isolated covariance test.
Reviewer FycL raised the six-month test window, tuning/reproducibility and
distributional realism. The authors added regime comparisons (A.4), covariance
substitutions (A.5), generated-distribution checks (A.6), ablations (A.7), and
path-count/L2 sensitivity (A.8). The rebuttal specifies M=1000 for distribution
bootstraps, resolving a detail omitted from the local manuscript. Those
intervals concern generated distribution statistics, not retrained-model ASR.
No documented ridge/pseudoinverse or cross-stock latent coupling was found.

Settings comparison and actions

1. Published selected hyperparameters — already matched.
   Table 12, p.25: horizon 20; embedding dim 16; DDIM reverse steps 30;
   paths 50; forward steps 200; US eta 0; gate cap 2; prior window 120;
   L2 coefficient .001. Table 13, p.26: 200 training epochs.

2. Fit dates — material mismatch corrected in a separate experiment.
   Table 2 and conference slides: 2013-01 through 2024-09-30.
   Original: fit only through 2023-12-31, with 2024 held out for validation.
   Follow-up: include fitting data through 2024-09-30; final epoch 200 is
   used without test selection. The author validation/checkpoint rule is
   undisclosed, so final-epoch selection is explicitly our assumption.
   Keep the 20-session origin grid and include a final complete target ending
   at the cutoff; omit targets with an unavailable inception-day return.
   The author's exact training stride is unknown.

3. Universe date and first-day eligibility — material differences corrected.
   Section 4.1, p.7: March 31, 2025 sector/capitalization selection and
   full-window history. Original used September 30, 2024.
   Follow-up uses March 31, 2025 S&P500 membership and capitalization, five
   full-history-eligible companies per sector. Eligibility uses observed
   closes and usable returns after inception; a new listing's first close
   is valid even when its preceding daily return is absent. This changes
   nine of 55 stocks. The exact author IDs and historical GICS snapshot
   remain unavailable. Choosing eligible stocks before the top-five ranking
   and using the primary listed security's capitalization are assumptions.
   Following the end-of-test selection rule is retrospective and must not
   be described as a point-in-time investable universe.

   Independent sanity check with the revised universe: equal-weight ASR
   0.915350, versus original 0.124451 and paper 0.82; annual vol 11.302%,
   versus paper 14.44%. This is closer in ASR but does not verify the data.
   No stock was chosen based on portfolio performance.

4. Features — correct explicit mismatches in Section 3.3/Table 1, p.5.
   Replace the selected-universe equal-weight market proxy with the actual
   CRSP S&P500 index level. Replace the rolling normalized log-price feature
   with a rolling normalized adjusted-close level. Estimate Kalman coefficients
   using raw daily log returns, as the stated regression specifies, instead
   of standardized returns. Downstream fitted feature scaling remains an
   explicit reconstruction choice. Dollar/gold proxies, revised macro data,
   lag conventions and handling of negative term spreads remain unresolved.

5. Noise objective — literal-equation interpretation, not author verified.
   Equations (1)-(2) display an expected squared vector norm plus beta times
   the squared parameter norm. Original F.mse_loss averages over the 20
   horizon entries, while Adam adds weight_decay=.002 after gradient clipping.
   Follow-up sums squared errors over horizon entries, averages over examples,
   and adds beta*sum(theta**2) explicitly before clipping. Relative loss/L2
   scaling changes by a factor of 20 before clipping. The prose calls this
   MSE and code is unavailable, so the author's reduction is still ambiguous.

6. Network and compute — unresolved, no invented architecture expansion.
   Paper describes a convolutional UNet but not widths, depth, normalization,
   optimizer, batch size or learning rate. Original is a compact 176194-
   parameter network. Paper US training is 2h01m on four RTX3080 GPUs; original
   training took about 27s on one RTX4060. Matching epoch counts does not
   establish equivalent training. Retain reconstructed topology and optimizer
   as explicit assumptions, not as recovered author settings.

7. Sampler and allocator — unresolved execution choices retained and labelled.
   Cosine schedule, target standardization, clean clipping at +/-8, independent
   initial shocks, ridge=.01, 2x gross cap and equal-weight fallback are not
   author-verified. Equation (4) gives a singular 55x55 covariance from 50
   centered samples. Removing ridge cannot produce a literal valid inverse.
   Higher path counts/shared shocks would change the published configuration;
   do not call them paper-matching corrections. The main follow-up retains
   the existing explicit repairs. Existing first-seed sensitivities are
   descriptive; they are not used to select a result.

8. Seed and timing — unresolved or already matched.
   Zero risk-free rate and daily annualized metrics match Section 4.1.
   Exact author seed, execution timing, rebalance calendar and price/dividend
   convention are unavailable. Retain conservative close-t signal,
   close-(t+1) execution, first return ending t+2, and signed buy-and-hold
   accounting. PyTorch 2.4.1 differs from the paper's 1.13.1/cu116 runtime.

Follow-up experiment

prepared data, source metadata and hashes. Seeds 20261120 and 20261121 are
the next two consecutive seeds, fixed before evaluating their model outputs.
Original checkpoints, protocol and all three original US results are retained.
The revised runs must not be pooled with the original three into a five-seed
estimate, because their setup differs. No claim of exact author reproduction
is warranted. Test outcomes from the original setup were already inspected;
this is a retrospective follow-up with paper-driven changes, not a pristine
pre-registered experiment. Final results will be in the experiment's REPORT.txt.
