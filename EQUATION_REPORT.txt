STABLE: reproduction and correctness verification
2026-11-17

STABLE has not been empirically reproduced in this workspace. The previous
105-stable project performed only source inspection. This follow-up implements
and executes a minimum numerical reconstruction of the specified equations.
It is sufficient to establish concrete mathematical defects and verify several
components against independent references. It is not sufficient to verify the
paper's reported financial performance or forecast accuracy.

The source is Soun, Y., Lee, H., Park, S., and Kang, U., STABLE: Shift-Tolerant
Allocation via Black-Litterman Using Conditional Diffusion Estimates, ICLR 2026.
The inspected 26-page author-hosted manuscript was downloaded on 2026-11-08:
https://datalab.snu.ac.kr/~ukang/papers/2026ICLR-stable.pdf
SHA256: 5258ee07088e473cecadee492828672ccbe36ceea1a559f890813bb697906605
Byte identity with the proceedings PDF remains unverified. Relevant equations
on pages 6 and 14 were also rendered and visually inspected in this follow-up.

Current release evidence remains insufficient. On 2026-11-17, the advertised
https://github.com/iclr26stable/iclr26stable returned HTTP 404 and the account's
public repository endpoint returned an empty list. A title/GitHub search and
the rendered coauthor homepage https://hosunglee.com/ did not identify a release.
OpenReview returned a browser verification page; that is an access limit, not
evidence that no supplemental material exists. The previous audit contains
additional lab-release searches. These observations do not prove that no private
or undiscovered implementation exists.

Fourteen deterministic checks passed under Python/NumPy versions recorded in
results/verification.json. Several checks deliberately verify counterexamples;
their PASS status must not be read as a correctness endorsement of STABLE.

1. The covariance inverse is undefined at the published US/China dimensions.
   Eq. (4) centers k cumulative-return samples. Therefore rank(Sigma_view) is
   at most k-1. Table 12 selects k=50, while Table 2 has S=55 for both markets:
   rank <=49 and nullity >=6, regardless of which learned generator is used.
   The numerical fixtures reproduced rank 49 for both 55-asset matrices.
   NumPy's raw inverse returned an infinity-norm identity residual of 109.07
   in the US fixture and raised LinAlgError in the China fixture. Floating-point
   inv() returning an array does not overcome the exact rank defect. Europe
   (37 assets) and Korea (44) are not forced singular by these dimensions.

   Explicit repair tested: C_ridge = C + lambda * trace(C)/S * I, with lambda
   in {1e-6, 1e-4, 1e-2, 1e-1}. All four fixtures became positive definite;
   solve residuals ranged from 1.20e-9 to 1.03e-14. This changes the estimator
   and the weights. It is not an author-verified correction, and lambda has not
   been selected using market performance. See results/ridge_sensitivity.csv.

2. The Sharpe formula requires a positivity condition omitted from the result.
   Write d = 1' Sigma_BL^-1 mu_BL. The normalized tangency direction is the
   maximum-Sharpe portfolio under the paper's sole budget constraint when d>0.
   When d<0, normalization reverses the direction and attains the lower
   Cauchy-Schwarz bound, i.e. minimum Sharpe. At d=0 it is undefined.

   A valid BL input pair produces posterior mean (-0.5,-1), covariance 0.5 I.
   The paper formula gives weights (1/3,2/3) and Sharpe -1.5811388301.
   Feasible weights (3,-2) sum to one and give Sharpe +0.1961161351.
   This is a mathematical counterexample under the stated budget-only
   constraints, not evidence that the authors actually encountered this case.
   The checked implementation rejects nonpositive/near-zero denominators.
   Choosing a constrained optimizer, cash asset or leverage cap would require
   a separately specified strategy. The guard does not invent one.

3. The Appendix A.3 equilibrium prior has a notable exact cancellation.
   That appendix sets mu_prior = Sigma_prior * w_equal. Combining Eqs. (6)-(8):
       w = normalize(w_equal + solve(Sigma_view, mu_view)).
   Consequently the prior covariance cancels from the final allocation when
   the view moments are held fixed, even though the posterior moments change.
   Changing the positive-definite prior across scales .01, 1 and 100 changed
   final weights by at most 8.33e-17. This limits an interpretation that the
   rolling prior covariance directly diversifies final weights under A.3.
   It is not an algebra error in the Gaussian update. A different prior-mean
   convention, extra constraints or different predictive covariance changes
   this conclusion and must be documented. Section 3.1's rolling-prior wording
   and Appendix A.3's explicit equilibrium construction need reconciliation.

4. Per-stock predictive distributions do not identify their joint covariance.
   The sample index in Eq. (4) must have a defined cross-asset coupling. Two
   fixtures with exactly the same marginal samples produce correlation +1
   or -1 solely by changing their pairing. Shared conditioning is not itself
   an explicit shared latent shock. Author code is needed to determine the
   actual coupling; this check does not prove their implementation is wrong.

5. Two other explanatory statements fail literal numerical checks.
   With Section 3.4's guidance equation, z=.5 gives the midpoint of macro and
   full-condition predictions, whereas z=1 gives the full-condition prediction.
   Appendix A.7's claim that z=.5 reduces to the full-condition denoiser needs
   clarification. Also, minimizing the noise MSE does not imply that predicted
   noise itself is N(0,I), as asserted in Section 3.3. In a Gaussian example
   with alpha_bar=.8, the population-optimal conditional-mean prediction has
   variance .2; simulation gives .200922. This observation does not invalidate
   standard DDIM training; it contradicts that particular justification.

The components that could be verified numerically behaved correctly. BL moments
match an independent whitened least-squares/SVD Gaussian reference with maximum
mean error 4.22e-15 and covariance error 3.33e-15. The positive-denominator
Sharpe solution agrees with an independent 200,001-point two-asset grid search.
Log-to-simple horizon compounding and unbiased sample moments agree with direct
reference calculations. Thirty deterministic DDIM steps recover an exact-noise
50 x 20 x 55 fixture within 5.51e-15. The Kalman implementation agrees with batch
Bayesian regression at Q=0, and changing future observations leaves past filtered
estimates unchanged. The integration fixture explicitly overrides its view mean
to test a known allocation solution; it is not a learned forecast experiment.

Published headline arithmetic is also recorded, separately from reproduction.
Against the strongest baseline in Table 3, the largest positive-baseline Sharpe
gain is 105.63% (Europe: 2.92 versus MOM 1.42), rather than the abstract's 122.9%.
The latter is approximately obtained against the weaker 1.31 baseline. Table 4
gives a maximum MSE reduction of 14.88% (China: 3.89 versus AEC-GAN 4.57), rather
than 15.7%. These comparisons use rounded published cells and concern those
tables; they do not establish the origin of the abstract figures. They require
author reconciliation, not replacement with claimed reproduced measurements.

What is still needed for an empirical reproduction: the exact 191 stock IDs
and price/macro panels with source and adjustment conventions; preprocessing
and availability timestamps; UNet architecture and optimizer/batch/lr settings;
diffusion schedule and return scaling; Kalman initialization/noise settings;
numeric seed; validation/search/checkpoint history; joint path construction;
covariance regularization; and executable portfolio constraints/accounting.
Earlier COVID/ZIRP experiments also need their own chronological training cuts
and label purging. The main split is train through 2024-09-30 and test from
2024-10-01 through 2025-03-31. The paper's 2025-03-31 universe selection is
retrospective and must be identified as such in any faithful replay.

No learned denoiser was trained, no market panel was substituted, and no Sharpe,
drawdown, volatility, forecast-error or profitability result was reproduced.
The existing 536 paper-target metric cells remain unverified. Running an
arbitrary substitute UNet or universe would not resolve those evidence gaps.
This work establishes that a literal uncorrected replay of the stated method
is not well-defined in the two 55-asset markets. It does not establish that the
authors' unavailable implementation or reported measurements are false.

Re-run instructions are in README.txt. The original source audit is preserved;
only this new stable-reproduction directory was authored for the follow-up.
