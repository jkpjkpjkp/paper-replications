EQUITY RECONSTRUCTION: CHOICES FROZEN BEFORE TEST RESULTS
Date: 2026-11-18. User authorized reconstruction using best judgment.

Scope
-----
Reconstruct the 79-input daily equity task and compare Set-Sequence with its
LongConv backbone, 2002-2021, five seeds each, annual refits on the preceding
eight years. This is fresh training on reconstructed data. It is not the
authors' released code/data, and does not cover the proprietary mortgage task,
synthetic contagion, other sequence baselines, or separately net-trained models.

Data and timing
---------------
CRSP CIZ daily 1989-2021 (1989-1993 warm-up) and FF3 daily factors are local
2026 downloads. Returns and RF are decimal, capitalization is thousands of USD,
Compustat money values are millions. PERMNO is the security identifier.
500 EQTY/COM stocks per year, with complete returns in all eight training years
AND the test year, ordered by capitalization on the last training date. This
retains the paper's ex-post test-completeness filter and hence its selection
using future availability. It is not a prospectively tradable universe rule.
Exact author vintage, quarter-cap convention and stock universe are unavailable.

At input close t all rolling calculations end at t; weights predict t+1 excess
returns. Never shift the label backward to the feature date. Aggregation and
ranks use the same selected 500 stocks at every date of that annual fit.

Accounting: use Chen's annual formulas, despite the target paper's broad
reference to quarterly characteristics. Standard consolidated domestic INDL,
STD, C, D records in USD, with official effective-dated CCM primary links.
Availability is fiscal date plus six calendar months plus one day; expire
after 18 months or CCM link expiry. No backfill. Annual lag calculations require
300-430 days between consecutive statements. The source is standard restated
Compustat, not a historical point-in-time vintage; lagging does not remove
later restatements. Annual updates are an explicit reconstruction choice.

Features
--------
The ordered 40 base inputs are in configs/equity_features.json. The 39 median
inputs follow Table 11 order. DP2A aliases DPI2A, ST_REV aliases ST_Rev.
Accounting formulas follow the reference appendix, with its working-capital
formula explicitly subtracting DLC. OP uses Compustat XINT for interest expense
(the appendix's text labels it TIE). Q follows the CEQ item code, despite the
reference's inconsistent prose description. Missing preferred stock, deferred
tax credits, debt components, minority interest, IVAO, R&D and advertising
are zero only where explicitly coded; other missing inputs remain missing.
Missing XSGA is zero for OP/OL, but remains missing for FC2Y. Positive book
equity is required for book-equity denominators. A2ME/BEME/CF2P/E2P use market
cap from the December immediately before the report's availability year.
Q and LME use current daily market cap. Exact formulas live in setseq/features.py.
The initial input audit found WCAPCH almost completely missing in the current
Compustat vintage. Before training, its CF input was reconstructed as the change
in ACT-LCT between consecutive annual balance sheets wherever WCAPCH is absent.
No zero-filled CF run was trained. Financial firms with missing ACT/LCT remain
missing and use the stated neutral-rank imputation.

Market windows: one month=21 trading sessions, one year=252, five years=1260.
r2_1 and ST_Rev both use the past 21-session cumulative return, as the reference
describes both as prior/lagged one-month returns. They are intentionally duplicate
inputs. r12_2: 231 sessions skipping 21; r12_7: 126 skipping 126; r36_13: 504
skipping 252. Ret_D1: daily return; Ret_W1: past five-session compounded return;
STD_W1: five-session population SD; Variance: 42-session population variance.
Rel2High: split-adjusted close / past 252-session maximum (minimum 120).
Vol: five-session sum of contemporaneous unadjusted share volume, choosing
Table 10's weekly label over the Appendix F daily-volume prose. Future cumulative
split factors are never used for absolute volume levels. LTurnover uses adjusted
past 21-session volume / current adjusted shares, where final-vintage split scale
cancels. Spread: average relative quoted spread over 21 sessions, minimum 10.
Invalid or crossed quotes are missing.
Beta: three-session log-excess-return correlation over 1260 sessions (minimum
750), multiplied by the 252-session daily log-excess-return volatility ratio
(minimum 120). Resid_Var: 42-session OLS residual variance on intercept+FF3,
requiring all observations. SUV: fit daily adjusted volume on intercept and
positive/negative return magnitudes using the preceding 60 sessions; standardize
today's residual by that fit's residual SD, then average over 21 sessions.
This specifies the reference's otherwise incomplete SUV regression horizon.

Ranks: average ties, (rank-1)/(n-1)-0.5; missing=0. A constant cross-section
becomes zero. RF is retained as a common daily numeric value, not ranked.
Median features are computed from raw available values before rank normalization.
Table 11 transformations use daily differences; log is defined only for positive
medians. Invalid transformed values are replaced with the TRAINING median,
centered by that median, divided by TRAINING population SD, and clipped to +/-10.
Zero-variance columns use scale 1. Test observations never fit preprocessing.

Model and training
------------------
Six residual Set-Sequence layers, hidden width 64, three-date trailing chunks,
phi output 5, pooled set summary 2. phi/rho/psi are two-linear-layer FFNs with
GELU, hidden width 64 and dropout 0.1. Mean aggregation; no stock-ID embedding.
LongConv has length 30, two channels, learned diagonal skip, GELU and GLU output
mixing. Kernel initialization SD .002 and soft threshold .003 follow upstream
config; kernel dropout is 0 following target Table 8. No smoothing. Residual
dropout 0.1 and post-LayerNorm. A linear encoder and per-date scalar head produce
signed weights, normalized to L1 exposure 1. No imposed dollar neutrality.
Baseline has the same encoder, six LongConv/residual/norm blocks and head,
but no phi/rho/psi. Parameters are reported; capacities are not equal.

AdamW LR .003, constant schedule; kernel weight decay .05, others zero; gradient
norm clip 1.0. 30 epochs. Nine randomly sampled 246-session training windows per
epoch (ceil(train days/246)), stratified by start position. This epoch definition
is a choice because the paper omits batches/epoch. All 500 units train jointly
under the portfolio Sharpe objective, including in the individual-backbone model.
Use the final epoch; no hyperparameter search or selection on the test period.
Initialization seeds 0-4; each fit derives its RNG seed as 10000*seed+test_year.
CUDA BF16 autocast for dense layers, float32 FFT/reductions. Runtime settings and
source hashes accompany every checkpoint. Chunked inference includes the full
causal receptive history (186 preceding dates for Set-Sequence, 174 for baseline).

Evaluation
----------
Concatenate all 20 chronological OOS years, then annualize with 252 sessions.
Report arithmetic annual mean excess return, population volatility, Sharpe,
market beta, average L1 turnover and fraction of negative-weight stocks.
Initial holdings are cash. At year boundaries turnover closes departing stocks
and aligns continuing stocks by PERMNO. No resetting to cash each year.
Paper reports five initialization means/SD; local sample seed SD uses ddof=1.
Predeclared approximate Sharpe bands are the paper's descriptive seed SD:
Set-Sequence +/-0.12 and LongConv +/-0.14. These are not confidence intervals
or tests of equivalence. All six metric differences are reported, even if a
single metric happens to match. A one-seed or short-period fit is preliminary.
Post-hoc net returns subtract .0005*turnover+.0001*short exposure. They are
diagnostics only, not reproduction of Table 9's separately net-trained model.
