ENVIRONMENT AND PROTOCOL LIMITS
Local GPU: NVIDIA RTX 4060, 8GB. Python 3.11.16, PyTorch 2.4.1+cu121,
NumPy 1.26.4, pandas 2.2.2, scipy 1.14.1, sklearn 1.5.2,
matplotlib 3.10.0. See reproduction/fresh_local/environment_manifest.json
for actual imports, data hash and deviations. PyTorch is read from an
existing environment without modifying that environment; the numerical
packages above are installed in a task-specific environment.
The author's requirements name NVIDIA PyTorch 2.6.0a0+ecf3bae40a.nv25.1.
That exact runtime was not reproduced. An attempted public PyTorch 2.6.0
installation was stopped after repeated slow download attempts; it was not
used to produce any result. The effect of the runtime difference is unknown.

The paper's Appendix D specifies five independent seeds, 100 maximum epochs,
patience 10 and validation grid search. The release specifies three runs,
30 epochs and patience 3, and seeds only once. Our primary numerical rerun
follows the released launcher. Missing original seed/search logs and this
protocol conflict prevent an exact published-protocol claim even if its
headline numbers were to match. Additional data universes, all learned
baselines, ablations and transaction-cost grids have not been freshly rerun.
There is also a reproducibility consequence of seeding only once: a change in
the stopping epoch can change the random-number state used by later runs,
through the intervening data-loader/shuffling draws. This is an inference
from the released execution order, not a demonstrated cause of the observed
performance gap. We did not replace the launcher with independently reseeded
runs or choose alternate seeds after seeing the test results.

FOCUSED 40-ASSET DISCREPANCY INVESTIGATION
Seven additional local diagnostic training runs are recorded separately.
Repeating the three primary 40-asset runs gives bitwise-identical checkpoints,
weights and metrics. Increasing first-run patience from 3 to 10 (max epochs
100) still selects the identical epoch-2 checkpoint, after stopping at epoch
12. Enabling matrix TF32 produces mean Sharpe 0.506198 and wealth 1.513423,

The original loader and all three frozen checkpoints reproduce all 63 fresh
allocation decisions, with maximum metric error below 8.61e-8. No source/data
drift was found in the public repository history. These checks rule out several
practical mistakes but do not uniquely identify the training-level cause.
Original GPU/runtime random streams and unreleased seed/search provenance
remain unresolved. See DISCREPANCY_40_ASSETS.txt for evidence and limitations.

BIAS ASSESSMENT
No direct future-price leak has been established in the inspected SIT code.
The principal unresolved issue is the point-in-time asset universe and
possible survivorship selection. Evaluation-date differences are confirmed;
net-cost claims need an additional holdings/turnover audit. Current evidence
does not justify excluding SIT for severe lookahead bias. The detailed audit
records observations, severity, uncertainty, evidence and proposed remedies.
