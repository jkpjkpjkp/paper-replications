Decision Making under the Exponential Family: Distributionally Robust Optimisation with Bayesian Ambiguity Sets

Condensed abstract

Introduces distributionally robust optimization using ambiguity sets derived from
Bayesian posterior expectations or posterior predictive distributions. Under stated
conditions, dual formulations turn worst-case decision problems into tractable
stochastic programs. Newsvendor and portfolio experiments demonstrate improved
robustness or faster computation relative to existing Bayesian robust optimization
methods.
