Vector-Quantized Discrete Latent Factors Meet Financial Priors: Dynamic Cross-Sectional Stock Ranking Prediction for Portfolio Construction

Condensed abstract

Combines expert financial factors with discrete latent factors learned through vector
quantization. The discrete representations suppress noise and guide a mixture of experts
that produces changing factor loadings. CSI300 and S&P500 experiments report improved
cross-sectional return prediction and portfolio performance while retaining factor-based
interpretability.
