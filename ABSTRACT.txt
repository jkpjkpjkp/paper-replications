Hermes: A Multi-Scale Spatial-Temporal Hypergraph Network for Stock Time Series Forecasting

Condensed abstract

Uses a hypergraph network to capture industry relationships, inter-industry lead-lag
effects, and stock dynamics at multiple temporal scales. Moving aggregation learns
changing lead-lag dependencies, while cross-scale message passing combines information
across resolutions. Experiments on real stock datasets report improved forecasting
accuracy over existing methods.
