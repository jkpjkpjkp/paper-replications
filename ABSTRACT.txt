Feature or Penalty? A Placement Diagnostic for Graph-Risk Signals in Learned Portfolio Decisions

Condensed abstract

Tests whether graph-derived risk signals work better as predictor features or explicit
portfolio penalties. A within-state permutation control separates the effect of penalty
placement from asset-specific graph ordering. Industry-portfolio and ETF experiments
attribute most aggregate downside-risk reduction to explicit penalties, with graph
ordering providing more selective benefits.
