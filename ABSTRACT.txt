Stable Alpha: Adversarial Invariant Representation Learning for Nonlinear Asset Pricing under Temporal Distribution Shifts

Condensed abstract

Introduces CASH, an asset-pricing framework that learns representations intended to
remain useful across changing market regimes. An information-theoretic adversarial
objective suppresses transient correlations, while a dynamic exposure network maps
invariant features into conditional factor prices. Experiments across stock markets
report improved out-of-sample forecasts and robustness to temporal shifts.
