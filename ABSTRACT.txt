AlphaQCM: Alpha Discovery in Finance with Distributional Reinforcement Learning

Condensed abstract

Treats the discovery of complementary formulaic alphas as a nonstationary, sparse-reward
decision process. A distributional reinforcement-learning method estimates expected
values and return quantiles, then uses quantiled conditional moments to estimate
variance and guide exploration. Financial experiments report stronger alpha discovery
than competing methods, particularly across large stock universes.
