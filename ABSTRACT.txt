Hierarchical Multi-Task Learning with Liquidity-Aware Signals for Stock Forecasting

Condensed abstract

Separates cross-stock dependencies from temporal dynamics, then jointly predicts
returns, volatility, and trading-volume signals through a gated mixture of experts. An
adaptive optimizer converts these forecasts into portfolios with transaction-cost and
liquidity constraints. CSI300 and CSI500 experiments report improved forecasts and more
effective portfolio allocation.
