TransAlpha: Lightweight Design Empowers Stock Return Forecasting

Condensed abstract

Introduces a lightweight transformer for intraday cross-sectional stock forecasting with
denoising, temporal attention gating, smart pooling, and a hybrid loss. Tests use 400
proprietary 15-minute alpha factors across the Chinese A-share market. Reported
comparisons assess predictive performance and portfolio profitability against existing
architectures.
