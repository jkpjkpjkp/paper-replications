FraudGNAM: Inherently Interpretable Spectral GNN for Graph Fraud Detection

Condensed abstract

Builds a spectral graph model whose fraud scores decompose into auditable feature,
interaction, and spectral-band contributions. Learnable multiscale filters capture
complex graph patterns while preserving additive interpretability. A graph-dependent
configuration rule reduces manual tuning, and experiments compare predictive performance
with strong graph-fraud baselines.
