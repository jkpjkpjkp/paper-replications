Retrieve the Right Signal: Decomposition-Guided Retrieval-Augmented Forecasting for Financial Markets

Condensed abstract

Separates financial series into causal trend and detrended components, then retrieves
similar historical patterns in the detrended space. Retrieved futures contribute both
direct forecasts and context tokens for the forecasting backbone. Experiments report
improved accuracy over retrieval, neural, and statistical baselines while avoiding
misleading matches driven mainly by signal levels.
