STABLE: Shift-Tolerant Allocation via Black-Litterman Using Conditional Diffusion Estimates

Condensed abstract

Uses macroeconomic context and stock-specific signals to generate regime-aware return
distributions with a conditional diffusion model. These forecasts and their covariance
structure become investor views in a Black-Litterman portfolio optimizer. Experiments
across equity markets report improved risk-adjusted returns, lower drawdowns, and better
return estimation under changing regimes.
