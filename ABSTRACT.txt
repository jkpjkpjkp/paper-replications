When Forecasting Gains Do Not Establish Alpha: An Evidence Audit of Financial Machine Learning

Condensed abstract

Audits whether financial prediction gains establish investable, risk-adjusted returns
under an explicit portfolio and cost protocol. One compatible LiMT transfer experiment
does not establish statistically significant positive alpha; this does not prove zero
profitability or baseline inferiority. The draft distinguishes catalogue coverage from
completed evidence and proposes more precise reporting standards.
