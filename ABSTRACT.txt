A Linearly Convergent Proximal Subgradient Algorithm for Sparse Portfolio Optimization with Transaction Cost

Condensed abstract

Formulates portfolio selection with both transaction costs and a fixed sparsity limit,
then replaces the difficult discrete constraint with an equivalent difference-of-convex
formulation under suitable penalties. A proximal subgradient algorithm has global and
linear convergence guarantees. Experiments compare its return-risk trade-off with
existing portfolio strategies.
