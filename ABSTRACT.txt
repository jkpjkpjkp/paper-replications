Expanding the Chaos: Neural Operator for Stochastic (Partial) Differential Equations

Condensed abstract

Combines Wiener-chaos expansions with neural operators to learn solutions of stochastic
differential and partial differential equations. Noise paths are projected onto Wick-
Hermite features, while neural networks learn the corresponding deterministic
coefficients. Experiments cover financial dynamics, stochastic PDEs, image generation,
and other applications, including settings without observed training noise paths.
