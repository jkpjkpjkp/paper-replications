Proximal Portfolio resolution audit - 2026-11-17

Outcome
Grade 2 is supported only for fresh optimization of the BCRP traditional
baseline on MSCI, over the complete original 1,043-session, 24-asset matrix,
at all three Table 5 transaction rates. No STCO/PSGA main-method mismatch was
resolved. The previous main-method failure remains in STATUS.txt and in the

The paper names BCRP as one of its three reference baselines. The fresh audit
computes all four data sets and all three costs, not just a chosen matching
cell. Only the MSCI BCRP experiment satisfies the stated rounding tolerance.
UBAH and BEST are data/accounting controls and are not the sole basis for
the new grade. BCRP uses hindsight to optimize a constant long-only portfolio
for gross log wealth, then applies that fixed portfolio at each cost rate;
it is a benchmark, not a deployable causal forecasting strategy.

Independent validation
The new script reads only the four original author .mat inputs, not archived
portfolios, wealth, predictions, or author outputs. Data and PDF SHA256 hashes
match the archived provenance exactly. Shapes are NYSE(O) 5651x36, NYSE(N)
6431x23, TSE 1259x88 and MSCI 1043x24.

BCRP optimization is independently performed with SLSQP and with a CVXPY/
Clarabel exponential-cone formulation. The latter uses log1p((x-1)@w), which
equals log(x@w) because sum(w)=1 and improves numerical conditioning.
For MSCI the maximum weight difference is 4.774e-8; the objective difference
is 8.432e-13 total log wealth and the maximum net-wealth difference across
the three costs is 4.962e-10. The concavity/first-order certificate bounds
SLSQP's total-log-wealth suboptimality by 5.913e-9. Clarabel reports optimal
for MSCI and TSE; its NYSE results report optimal_inaccurate, but agree with
the separately certified SLSQP solutions to at most 2.943e-6 net wealth.
The NYSE warnings are retained in the run log; no solver status alone was
treated as a certificate or a reason to change the paper tolerance.

Transaction fractions independently solve
  theta + gamma * sum(abs(previous_drifted_weights-theta*w)) = 1
with Brent's root algorithm. Initial holdings are zero, so the initial
purchase costs 1/(1+gamma); no terminal liquidation cost is added. Maximum
equation residual across all audited BCRP paths is 3.22e-15. BEST paths
also agree with their closed-form buy-and-hold identities to relative 1e-10.
SLSQP first-order, independent objective, self-financing and shape assertions
all passed in the final full run. Full precision checks are in summary.json.

Main-method failure and attempted resolution
The archive still contains 288 strategy/rate/window backtests and 20
sensitivity runs. Main STCO settings match 0/24 Table 5 wealth cells within
1%: NYSE(N) STCO2 at window 4 and 0.25% cost gives 3153.9 versus 600000;
TSE STCO2 at zero cost gives 389.1 versus 95.92. Window-5 sensitivities did
not establish a representative match. BCRP on the other three datasets also
fails the tighter two-decimal rounding comparison.

Current GitHub main history was checked on 2026-11-17:
https://github.com/Ting221/STCO/commits/main/
It still ends at a2e04730cbdfb6ccb0017456d4f835a4d7446ec3 (Mar 31, 2026).
The supplied STCO1_run.m calls absent DENRPO1_fun; STCO2_run.m declares
DENRPO2_run and calls absent DENRPO2_fun. Supplied functions implement a
different eta/tao/scalar-multiplier scheme, without the K/c/top-K PSGA loop.
No corrected implementation was found in this release history.

Algorithm 2 line 6 uses the moving inner w^j anchor, whereas the quadratic
in subproblem (12) requires the fixed outer anchor. Four sampled MSCI
subproblems at zero-based days 1,100,500,1042 were independently re-evaluated
with both readings, tolerance 1e-8, rho=.618 and alpha=.0025. The fixed-anchor
version differs from the exact convex prox by at most 8.324e-7 in a weight;
the moving-anchor version differs by up to 0.958189181. These samples use
archived drifted holdings, so they are diagnostics, not new backtest evidence.
The original pseudocode also does not specify inner d/w initialization.

A literal moving-anchor MSCI STCO1 0.25% preflight was attempted with inner
w,d initialized from each outer iterate and mu reset. The first attempt was
manually stopped after several minutes without a completed 50-day checkpoint.
A bounded diagnostic then reached 100 outer iterations and 438698 total
inner iterations on the first rebalance (day index 1) without satisfying the
outer stopping condition. The diagnostic caps were 100 outer/100000 inner,
smaller than the paper's 1e8; it is an unfinished verification, not a result
showing method failure. No full-data numerical comparison is claimed from it.

Remaining requirements are executable PSGA experiment code, clarification of
the quadratic anchor and inner initialization/stopping rules, and STCO2's
window. Other competitor implementations and full-paper claims remain open.
WRONG.txt separates the verified specification/source inconsistencies from
uncertain author intent and from the numerical mismatches themselves.
