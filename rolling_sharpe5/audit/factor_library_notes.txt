Data:
- 1,200 monthly observations, Jan 1926 to Dec 2025 inclusive, 212 predictors.
- Percent units: divide by 100 for decimal returns.
- These are signed original-paper long-short portfolio returns. Do not
  multiply by Sign again. Do not subtract the risk-free rate from a
  zero-investment long-short factor a second time.
- Date is last trading day of RETURN month, not formation month.
- Unique consecutive months; no infinities. 78,871 missing factor-months
  are genuine coverage gaps and must not be replaced with zero.
- Predictor metadata include publication Year, original sample dates,
  portfolio construction, Sign, Stock Weight, and Detailed Definition.
  All 212 predictors have publication Year (range 1973-2016).
- Original-paper universe is 184 equally weighted and 28 value weighted.
- Alternative decile value-weighted file has 179 predictors, not 212.
- Locally available long-format tables have only signalname, port, date,
  ret, signallag, Nlong, Nshort. There are no holding-level weights or
  turnover series. Cannot compute 5bp multiplied by actual trading volume
  from these return files. Do not treat signal updating period as turnover;
  the release notes say EW/VW weights are enforced monthly.
- Local package does not include daily factor returns or firm-level signals;
  official SOURCE_README lists separate downloads for these.
- Options signals end Dec 2022; governance in 2007; patent signals around
  2006; ProbInformedTrading in 2012. Check individual nonmissing endpoints.

Strict annual IID feasibility calculation:
- Target year y uses exactly y-5, ..., y-1, with 12 observations in EACH.
- Test H0 mean<=0 versus >0 using Student t with 11 df; annual Sharpe
  equals t-stat when n=12 monthly returns. This tests Sharpe>0 under IID.
- One-sided p<0.05 requires annual Sharpe>1.7959 every one of five years.
- Conventional two-sided p<0.05 and mean>0 requires Sharpe>2.2010 each year.
- No multiple-testing correction, no costs in this quick screening audit.
- One-sided: all 96 target years 1931-2026, 46 nonempty years, 148 total
  selected factor-years; max pool size 8. No selected factors in 2011-2026.
- Two-sided: 32 nonempty years, 61 selected factor-years; max size 5.
  Latest nonempty year is 2008; no selected factors in 2009-2026.
- Requiring publication Year<target year reduces one-sided to 17 nonempty
  years and 26 factor-years; two-sided to 11 years and 14 factor-years.
  Latest published-factor nonempty year is 2008 under either test.
- Therefore the 2026 pool from 2021-2025 is empty under both IID tests.
- An empty factor pool must be reported as such, not silently converted
  into a pooled 60-month test or a relaxed significance threshold.

Inference cautions:
- IID annual tests are feasibility diagnostics, not robust final inference.
  HAC estimates with only 12 observations are sensitive to lag choice.
- 212 predictors were historically selected from the literature and their
  signs use original-paper findings. Rolling return selection alone does
  not remove factor discovery/look-ahead bias. A publication restriction
  should be an explicit robustness analysis or primary availability rule.
- This is a revised 2026 vintage, not vintage-as-of-each-year input data.
  Revision and survivorship effects remain even with lagged selection.
- Annual testing has low power for moderate Sharpe ratios. Requiring five
  consecutive significant years selects unusually strong historical runs.
- Incremental mean/alpha tests of paper factors and portfolio combination
  effects are distinct questions. Empty pool years provide no factor
  spanning controls; standalone returns are not evidence of incremental
  alpha conditional on a nonempty pool.
