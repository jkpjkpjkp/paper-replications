Independent statistical review of proposed rolling-year factor addition design
2026-11-03

1. Primary factor selection is well defined: two-sided p<0.05 and positive
   mean in EACH prior calendar year, 12 valid monthly observations per year;
   publication Year < target year; original paper direction retained. IID
   annual tests are primary; HAC1 with finite-sample correction and t(11)
   inference is robustness. Five prior years end Dec y-1. Never use current
   returns, endpoint availability, or ex-post success to change selection.

2. Annual Sharpe>0 is equivalent to mean>0 when volatility>0, so a test of
   the mean is appropriate for the zero-Sharpe null. The reported Sharpe
   itself should remain sqrt(12)*mean/sample_std; do not label the HAC t as
   Sharpe. HAC t and IID annual Sharpe differ. A HAC test with n=12 is only
   a small-sample sensitivity analysis, not proof of well-calibrated size.

3. Pooled60 selection is a different economic question, not a robustness
   substitute for requiring five individually significant years. Report it
   separately if requested. Annual IID and annual HAC1 2026 pool counts are
   0; pooled60 IID/HAC3 counts are 31/32 (two-sided p<0.05, positive mean).
   Pooled unrestricted historical k can exceed 100. Even publication-filtered
   k reaches 52/56 with only 60 training months: unconstrained spanning OLS
   is unstable long before k reaches n. Use aggregate EW-pool regression
   or label regularized residuals exploratory; do not call this full spanning.

4. Empty selected pools must contribute baseline excess return 0 (cash) to
   the primary sequence; do not drop those years. Their hedge residual is
   simply the paper factor's return. Report number of empty/nonempty years
   and nonempty-year results as descriptive diagnostics: otherwise an
   apparent incremental-alpha test is mostly a standalone mean-return test.

5. Define 'add factor' explicitly. Comparing paper return with the EW pool
   is not itself the realized effect of adding it. If existing k factors
   are each given equal weight and the paper factor gets one additional
   equal sleeve, augmented=(k*baseline+paper)/(k+1); incremental
   augmented-baseline=(paper-baseline)/(k+1), for k>0. If k=0, use baseline=0
   and augmented=paper. A 50/50 blend answers a different question. State
   capital/gross-exposure normalization, especially because conventional
   zoo long-short factors are +1 long/-1 short.

6. For rolling60-beta residuals, fit the training regression with intercept,
   but apply out-of-sample h_t=paper_t-beta_hat'factor_t, WITHOUT subtracting
   the fitted intercept. The mean of h tests remaining expected return.
   Subtracting alpha_hat too gives forecast error and does not estimate
   the usual factor-adjusted alpha. Do not fit betas on current-year data.
   Audit n, rank, condition number, max absolute beta, and hedge exposure.

7. Returns on beta hedges or factor combinations are only partially costed:
   paper trades may have 5bp costs, but zoo returns are gross and no local
   weights/turnover exist. Label each result precisely, e.g. 'paper factor
   net of 5bp versus gross zoo controls'. Regression alpha is not proof of
   net implementable factor-hedged P&L. Rebalancing across factor sleeves
   and shorts' borrow/financing are also distinct from paper trade costs.
   Never deduct a constant 5bp from every day or month unless that is the
   specified turnover convention. Cost must follow actual trading notional.

8. Aggregate the daily paper strategy consistently with its capital and
   rebalancing convention; compare monthly dates by Period('M'), since last
   calendar dates may differ from last trading dates. Ensure both series
   use decimal or percent consistently. Last valid prior 60 months must be
   calendar-complete; no row-count windows that jump across missing months.

9. Primary inference should report annualized arithmetic mean/alpha,
   monthly HAC CI, t, p, N, realized volatility/Sharpe. Annualization of the
   mean and its CI uses x12, volatility sqrt(12); it does not turn the mean
   into CAGR. Because adaptive rolling regressions overlap training windows,
   HAC3 alone can understate longer dependence. Include HAC6/HAC12
   sensitivity; a full-pipeline moving/block bootstrap would better address
   estimated weights and selection if a formal robust inference claim is
   desired. Do not choose bandwidth by which one makes p significant.

10. If multiple paper factors/specifications are tested as discoveries,
    distinguish the prespecified primary from exploration and report an
    appropriate multiple-testing adjustment (e.g. Holm for a defined family).
    The strict five-year intersection rule is not automatically a 212-factor
    multiple-testing correction. The factor library itself embeds literature
    discovery and revised-vintage selection; Year filters mitigate only
    publication availability, not all discovery and revision biases.

11. 'Out of sample' should mean specific choices are learned before the
    holding year. It does not automatically mean the paper's newly proposed
    factor is out of its original discovery sample or that revised 2026
    factor series were available in real time. Clearly distinguish these.
