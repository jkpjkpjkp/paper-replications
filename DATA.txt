Missing characteristics and histories
------------------------------------
The 209 source signals yield 202 nonconstant observed training signals. Seven
excluded signals: Activism1, Activism2, ChNAnalyst, Governance, PatentsRD,
ProbInformedTrading, RDcap. Selection uses training data only, not test outcomes.
Source evidence: ../.replication-local/artifacts/ordered-reproductions/results/
cz-preflight.json (per-feature observed counts and training eligibility).

Observed values are cross-sectionally percentile-ranked within the feature month
using average ties, then transformed as 2*rank-1. Missing values are set to 0,
a neutral rank, NOT an observed raw characteristic of zero. No future-month
values are used to fill gaps. A normalized zero can also be an observed median;
there is no extra missingness indicator. This is an adaptation assumption.

The model uses 16 consecutive calendar months ending at the row's feature month.
Absent historical stock-month rows (e.g. new listings or previous exclusion by
the size screen) receive an all-zero neutral vector. Histories are aligned by
PERMNO and calendar month; gaps are never compressed or backfilled. These gaps
need not mean the raw characteristic was unavailable: the prepared panel also
omits stocks below the size screen. The current eligible universe remains whole
for cross-sectional denoising, and all test stocks receive predictions.

Missing targets would stop this frozen run; they are never silently zero-filled.
The source is a current-vintage CZ/CRSP release with publication lags, not a
historically archived as-published vintage. Missingness and revisions limit
interpretation even though no future labels enter inputs or universe selection.

Evaluation and remaining original-paper inputs
----------------------------------------------
The adaptation uses raw monthly total returns, whole monthly cross sections,
the reconstructed TransAlpha model/loss, 16 monthly history slots, three fixed
seeds (0/1/2), and a linear control. Checkpoints use validation IC only.
The primary portfolio is market-cap-weighted D10-D1 (+1/-1 exposure), with 10bp
per dollar traded including initial and final trades. FF5+UMD regressions report
annual alpha, HAC t, 95% CI and p (Bartlett 6 lags, finite-sample correction).
Costs use a gross-weight turnover ledger; borrowing, financing, price impact,
spread heterogeneity and taxes are not modeled. This is not the daily CSI
portfolio or the paper's intraday execution protocol.

Still absent: the paper's private anonymous 400-factor 15-minute panel, aligned
stock identifiers/returns, exact preprocessing, chosen hyperparameters and full

CZ202 cannot reconstruct those proprietary factors or CSI2000/full-market tests.
Thus this run is a requested method adaptation, not an original Table 3 numerical
replication. Original-paper error/tolerance is not applicable to US monthly
