Verified primary sources for the announcement-cycle overlay study
Checked 2026-11-02. This is a methods/source note, not a replication of any
paper's reported returns. Live master code may differ from the frozen 2026.10
input release; the local download receipts and SignalDoc hash define our data.

1. Open Source Asset Pricing: SignalDoc
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OpenSourceAP/CrossSection/master/SignalDoc.csv
Primary dataset documentation. Local frozen copy:
/home/jkp/Téléchargements/Chen_Zimmermann_Factor_Zoo_2026_10/SignalDoc.csv
Verified signs: MS +1; CompEquIss -1; InvestPPEInv -1; Cash +1;
RIO_Disp +1; RDIPO -1. Publication years: 2005, 2006, 2008, 2012,
2005, 2006 respectively. CompEquIss measures five-year equity-value growth
minus stock return; InvestPPEInv measures changes in equipment and inventory
over lagged assets; Cash is quarterly cash/short-term investments over assets;
RIO_Disp is lagged institutional-ownership rank within high disagreement.
The Return field is a collected original-paper statistic, with different
portfolio/regression conventions by row; it is not a common net-cost return.

2. Open Source Asset Pricing: FAQ
https://www.openassetpricing.com/faq/
Verified directly. Monthly predictor dates are month-end information and
predict subsequent returns. The FAQ explains conservative accounting lags,
why some predictor dates extend into the future, the importance of portfolio
construction, and difficulties with tied/discrete characteristics. Therefore
we use the exact previous calendar month's snapshot and do not forward-fill
absent snapshots. Source construction lags do not establish a complete
point-in-time restatement audit.

3. OpenSourceAP MS implementation
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OpenSourceAP/CrossSection/master/Signals/pyCode/Predictors/MS.py
Verified directly through Chrome. The construction restricts to the lowest
book-to-market quintile, builds eight indicators, and clips the final score
to 1 through 6. Its comments explicitly discuss difficult mixed-frequency
timing and reproduction details. Our factor baseline uses these available
characteristics rather than claiming exact original-paper replication.

4. OpenSourceAP RDIPO implementation
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OpenSourceAP/CrossSection/master/Signals/pyCode/Predictors/RDIPO.py
Verified directly through Chrome. RDIPO equals one for firms more than six
and at most 36 months after IPO with R&D expense exactly zero; it is zero
otherwise. Our signed economic comparison shorts raw1 and buys raw0.
This binary distinction must not be replaced by arbitrary percentile-tie
splitting. The regular-announcement universe substantially reduces IPO supply.

5. Boyd, Busseti, Diamond, Kahn, Koh, Nystrup and Speth (2017),
Multi-Period Trading via Convex Optimization
https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/papers/cvx_portfolio.html
Verified author-hosted paper page. Provides an explicit expected-return,
risk, trading-cost and holding-cost optimization framework. It does not
provide predictive alpha. Our constrained mean-variance/no-trade-band design
is a simplified implementation motivated by that framework, not a claim
that its full multi-period optimizer has been reproduced.

6. Gu, Kelly and Xiu (2020), Empirical Asset Pricing via Machine Learning
https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa009
Verified original journal search text and NBER working-paper record. Uses
separate temporally ordered training, validation and testing periods; tuning
uses validation and is not itself untouched out-of-sample evidence. Motivates
linear/regularized/nonlinear comparisons with common information sets and
explicit separation of model fitting, tuning and evaluation. Its reported
ML performance is not evidence for this announcement-cycle signal.

Interpretation of the user's historical net monthly return ranges
The supplied 80-100/60-80/etc. basis-point ranges have no specified sample,
cost model, borrow availability, weighting, exposure or portfolio definition.
They have not been verified as common after-cost returns and are not inputs,
targets or promised performance in this study. We estimate all six baselines
from the same local stock returns, the same available information and the
same cost scenarios used by their augmented models. Cost scenarios are not
observed historical implementation costs. Positive raw increments do not
automatically constitute risk-adjusted or deployable alpha.
