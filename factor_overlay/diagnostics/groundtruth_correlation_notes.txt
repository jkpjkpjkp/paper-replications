All-eligible groundtruth diagnostics, computed without refitting any model.

The dataset contains1,251,064 five-session outcome labels on every fifth
market session, with no factor-tail or training-sample restriction. All fields
used as predictors retain their historical availability. Future prices enter
only the return labels. Dates whose five-session horizon crosses a calendar
boundary are purged consistently with the earlier direct-horizon study.

Correlations are cross-sectional within each date, so shared market-return
levels and time trends do not create the reported signal/return correlations.
The period statistic is an equal-date average, with HAC12 on the weekly grid.
A correlation of0.005 is one-half of one percent on the correlation scale;
it is not a0.5% return, nor a directly investable return forecast.

Fixed-clock Pearson correlation with five-day returns averages0.00457 before
2018 and0.00215 afterward. Its Spearman correlation is nearly unchanged:
0.00354 and0.00342. Thus Pearson weakening alone does not prove a regime
break. MS is also approximately stable, CompEquIss strengthens, and
InvestPPEInv/RIO_Disp turn negative. Broad claims that all signals became
worse after2018 are not supported by this matrix.

Each pair uses its own finite observations. MS and RIO_Disp have embedded
source screens, while RDIPO is a very unbalanced binary indicator. The
resulting pairwise matrix need not be positive semidefinite and should not
be fed directly into a portfolio covariance optimizer. Intervals are
unadjusted descriptive intervals across a post-result chosen cutoff.

Checks:105 independently expressed fixed-share labels matched exactly;
15,633 overlapping2015 stock-dates matched existing sampled train5 labels
within float32 rounding (maximum8.13e-8); independent scipy Pearson and
Spearman calculations matched a displayed MS/return5 date.
