STABLE specification concerns — reviewed 2026-11-17

These concern the displayed method, not a claim that the unavailable author
implementation used an incorrect numerical procedure. The reconstruction's
poor Sharpe alone is not evidence of a flaw in the original experiment.

Verified rank deficiency: Equation (4), p.6, estimates covariance from k centered
sample vectors. Rank is at most k-1. Tables 2 and 12 specify S=55 and k=50 for
US/China, hence rank <=49 and the ordinary view-covariance inverse in Appendix
A.3 does not exist. Ridge/pseudoinverse/joint-sampling resolution is not specified
in the inspected paper or public replies. See EQUATION_REPORT.txt, results/
covariance_rank.csv and evidence/paper-page-06.png.

Missing positivity condition: Appendix A.3's budget-normalized tangency formula
maximizes Sharpe only if 1' Sigma_BL^-1 mu_BL > 0. Negative normalization flips
the maximizing direction; zero normalization is undefined. Existing numerical
counterexample and independent checks are recorded in EQUATION_REPORT.txt.
Whether the author's data encountered such cases is unknown.

Unspecified joint sampling: stock-wise conditional distributions alone do not
determine cross-stock covariance. A shared conditioning vector does not specify
shared random shocks. The inspected description leaves sample pairing/coupling
unresolved; this is a reproducibility gap, not proof of incorrect author code.

Retrospective universe: Section 4.1 selects using 2025-03-31 GICS/capitalization
and full-history availability, although the test starts 2024-10-01. This uses
end-of-test universe information. It prevents interpreting this universe choice
as a point-in-time strategy. The magnitude/direction of any selection effect
on the author's result is not established. The follow-up explicitly follows
the reported date while labelling this limitation.
