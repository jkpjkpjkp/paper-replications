The synthetic contagion baseline has inconsistent update equations. This is

Section 5 defines lambda_next = beta*lambda + alpha*N, with alpha=4.
The summary equation on p.19 preserves alpha in both expected and observed
default fractions. But the displayed prediction and correction on p.20 are
  lambda_prediction = beta*lambda + p
  lambda_next = lambda_prediction + K*(N_observed-p).
At full observation K=1 these imply beta*lambda+N, missing alpha.
With beta=.5, lambda=.01, and N=.002, the first rule gives .013 and the
second .007. The discrepancy is .006, and is not a stochastic sampling error.
The p.20 caption also states alpha=4, so alpha=1 is not a stated resolution.

If implemented literally, the p.20 rule would underweight the observed-default
contribution by four at full observation, defeating the claimed full-observation
tracking property. This is a conditional methodological concern. It could be a
typesetting omission; no author implementation was located to distinguish it.
The paper's trained Set-Sequence scores are not refuted by this check.
