Verified released-demo timing issue; applicability to the paper is unresolved.

The released finetune/qlib_test.py configures deal_price="open" together with
limit_threshold=0.095. Qlib 0.9.7's Exchange._update_limit (exchange.py:289-291)
constructs those limit flags from the same day's $change. In the community-data
reconstruction this field is the current close / previous close - 1. Therefore
the primary demo-based backtest can use information from the day's close to
decide whether an order fills at that day's open. This is a lookahead mechanism
in the tradability filter, even though the prediction signal uses only the
previous close's context. It can affect buy and sell execution in either direction.

The paper's Appendix D, Investment Simulation Setup, states that simulations
use Qlib, top-k/drop-n, a five-day minimum hold and 0.15% transaction costs.
It does not establish these exact execution/limit settings. This finding does
NOT prove that the published Table 10 used the same problematic combination.
The released code explicitly describes itself as a simplified finetuning demo.
Do not equate the reconstructed strategy's numerical match, if any, with a
verified causal investment result.
