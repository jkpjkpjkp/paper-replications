Methodological vulnerabilities to investigate — assessed 2026-11-17

Scope: mathematical properties of the published equations and measurements of
the independent local reconstruction. These do not establish that the authors'
implementation has the same behavior, that their reported results are false,
or that the paper has lookahead bias. No author implementation was available
in the inspected local evidence.

1. The ranking objective can reward shrinking predictions without better ranking.
Paper Section 3.7, Eq.24 defines the sum of max(0, -prediction_difference *
return_difference). For predictions p and any positive c, this ranking term
satisfies R(c*p) = c*R(p), while Pearson IC is unchanged. Constant predictions
have zero ranking loss. Thus this term alone does not distinguish a useful
ordering from ties; shrinking scores can reduce it without improving IC.
MSE supplies a competing constraint, so this is not proof that constant
predictions minimize the combined objective.

With mean MSE, the written pair sum also changes relative weighting with
universe size: for 474 valid stocks, sum versus mean differs by 224,676.
Local Figure 3 runs have training ranking/MSE ratios of 2,042, 12,381 and
11,686 at their selected epochs. The local loss-based checkpoint rule is an
assumption; its mismatch with IC must not be attributed to author selection.
Earlier mean-pair runs still failed (three-seed test IC -0.002929), so changing
reduction alone is not an established remedy.

2. Inverse-distance fusion is undefined for coincident off-diagonal embeddings.
Paper Eqs.17–18 take the reciprocal of a PSD squared distance before softmax.
Distinct nodes can have identical embeddings, or a PSD metric can have a
nullspace, making that distance zero. A stabilizer is needed but is not given
in these equations. The reconstruction adds a 1e-6 floor. On the first 32
training target dates at the Figure 3 checkpoints, 97.3–99.5% of softmax columns
have maximum weight above 0.999. This establishes concentrated local routing,
not that it caused the test failure or that author routing behaves identically.

Remedies to test: recover author preprocessing/configuration; separately compare
loss weighting, validation-IC checkpoint selection, and stabilized fusion on
training/validation data. Retain current outputs and treat the already inspected
test period as a replication diagnostic, not an untouched tuning benchmark.
