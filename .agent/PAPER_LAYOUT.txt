 (i) If θ = 0, then {wk : k ∈ N} converges to w∗ finitely;

(ii) If θ ∈ (0, 12 ], then ∥wk − w∗ ∥2 ≤ a0 bk0 for any k ≥ k0 with k0 > 0, a0 > 0, b0 ∈ (0, 1);

(iii) If θ ∈ ( 21 , 1), then ∥wk − w∗ ∥2 ≤ a1 k −(1−θ)/(2θ−1) for any k ≥ k1 with some k1 > 0 and a1 > 0.

Then, Theorem 3.13 is a direct consequence of Theorem 3.12, Proposition A.4 and Proposition 3.11.
