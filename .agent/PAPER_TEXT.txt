Kronos: A Foundation Model for the Language of Financial Markets
Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.02739v1
Full article: https://arxiv.org/html/2508.02739v1

Kronos models financial candlestick sequences with a discrete tokenizer and an
autoregressive transformer. Its tokenizer compresses price and trading-activity
information into hierarchical tokens, which the predictor models over time.
The paper evaluates forecasting, synthetic sequence generation, and investment
simulations, alongside architecture ablations and inference-sampling choices.

Navigation for future work:
- Section 3: tokenization, hierarchical modeling, pretraining and inference.
- Section 4: experimental setup, main results and ablations.
- Appendix B: datasets, cleaning and missing observations.
- Appendix C: architecture, training and inference configurations.
- Appendix D: evaluation tasks, baselines, metrics, portfolio rules and costs.
- Appendices E-F: additional and full experimental results.

Match the paper version and model checkpoint before comparing results. The local
folder provides reduced pretrained inference; consult STATUS.txt and README.md
for its actual scope. The original article is the reference for equations,
numeric tables, figures and complete protocol details.
