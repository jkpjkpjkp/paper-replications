Corporate customer-graph anomaly study

Concise study summary

Studies anomalies in disclosed corporate customer relationships using chronological
graph scores and filing dates. The research plan distinguishes contemporaneous
association from forward prediction and examines links to subsequent stock returns only
where timing and identifiers support that analysis. Graph anomaly scores themselves do
not use return outcomes.
