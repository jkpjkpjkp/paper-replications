557   A.2. Experimental Setup
558   The experiments were implemented in Python, using PyTorch for the predictive model and CVXPY layers for the optimization
559   module (Agrawal et al., 2019), which enabled differentiation through the portfolio optimization step and thus an end-to-end
560   decision-focused learning pipeline. The prediction network consists of two fully connected hidden layers with 256 and 128
561   neurons, respectively, trained with a learning rate of 0.001 and a mini-batch size of 64. The combined loss uses a weighting
562   parameter α = 0.5 to balance prediction accuracy and decision quality, which we found to improve training stability and
563   efficiency. In the differentiable top-k selection layer, the bisection procedure is run for 32 iterations. All experiments were
564   carried out on a machine equipped with an Intel Xeon Silver 4310 CPU (@2.10GHz) and an NVIDIA RTX A6000 GPU
565   with 48GB of memory, providing sufficient computational resources to train the model and solve the embedded optimization
566   problems.
