Reconstruction decisions and evidence boundaries (2026-11-06)
============================================================

Source identification
---------------------
The exact paper is the nine-page IJCAI 2026 publication by Xiao Yang of
Carleton University. The publisher page and PDF were read; pages 3-5 were
rendered to inspect equations, Algorithm 1, and experimental settings.
paper.pdf is the downloaded source. paper.txt is a layout-preserving
extraction. publisher-page.txt is the publisher's accessible full text.
Search receipts preserve the search queries and returned URLs.

Directly specified by Sections 3.1-3.4
-------------------------------------
N synchronized stocks, L=16, D=400 proprietary factors at 15-minute frequency;
hidden dimension d=64. CSD subtracts the cross-sectional stock mean for
each factor and time, uses factor-wise alpha/beta scales and threshold
tau=1e-3, and leaves small alpha residuals unscaled. The projection is
Linear -> LayerNorm -> ReLU -> Dropout. Scalar temporal scores are
softmax-normalized over time, multiplied into all hidden states. SP
concatenates the gated last state and temporal elementwise maximum.
The output is a two-layer MLP. No positional encoding or self-attention
block is added. The prose includes dropout; Algorithm 1 omits it. We use
the prose equation. Factor scales start at one (initialization unspecified).

Loss: 0.4 rank + 0.05 Huber + 0.4 correlation + 0.1 product + 0.05 L2.
Top/bottom target quantiles are 20%; margin 0.1; Huber delta 1.0;
L2=1e-5 * sum of squared model parameters. L2 is consequently multiplied
by 0.05 again, as written in Section 3.3. Optimizer weight decay is zero
to avoid an additional penalty. Correlation denominators are floored for
constant vectors. All loss terms are reduced per cross section, then
averaged across dates. Targets are raw decimal returns, not normalized.

The paper uses ordinal ranks, not a differentiable rank surrogate. Hard
ranks are evaluated with stable ordering and detached. Product loss still
differentiates through its Pearson factor. Ties in training ranks use
stable ordinal ranks; evaluation uses standard average ranks for Spearman.
The top/bottom pairing is unspecified: we average the hinge over every
top-bottom stock pair, rather than arbitrary one-to-one pairing or a
hinge on mean returns. This is a disclosed interpretation, not recovered
author code. Vectorized values and gradients are tested against separate
per-date computations, including variable stock and label masks.

Unspecified settings resolved before test evaluation
---------------------------------------------------
Head width=64 with ReLU between layers, dropout=0.1; Adam initial lr=1e-3,
lr/sqrt(epoch) schedule; 30 maximum epochs, patience 5 on mean validation
IC; four complete cross sections per optimizer step. Dates are shuffled,
stocks remain synchronized. Three seeds (0,1,2); full precision and
deterministic PyTorch operations. Paper says a validation grid search
was used but does not publish the grid, chosen rate/dropout/batch size,
epochs, stopping rule, seeds, head width, or target scale beyond raw returns.
CSI500/1000 linear controls run on CPU; neural fits and CSI300 controls
run on CUDA. Device choices affect runtime, not the fixed training objective
or statistical selection. Floating-point results can vary across devices.
This package fixes reconstruction choices instead of asserting they were
the author's choices. PyTorch 2.4.1/CUDA12.1 on RTX4060 differs from the
paper's stated PyTorch2.7.0/CUDA11.8 and four RTX4090s.
Other workspace jobs share the GPU. Logged durations are operational
records, not controlled evidence of speed relative to other architectures.

Ablations: no-CSD bypasses denoising; no-TAG uses identity gating; no-SP
uses temporal mean pooling. The paper reports only removal of CSD and
TAG, without replacement specifications. The SP removal is an extra
diagnostic. The independent linear control maps the last feature vector
to a scalar and uses the same optimizer/loss/validation protocol. It is
not the DLinear, RLinear, or other published baseline.

Daily data adaptation
---------------------
The local, pinned community dataset contains daily OHLCV/VWAP and a price
adjustment factor, not proprietary intraday alphas. We compute the public
Qlib Alpha158 expressions, replacing every price reference by raw price
times the positive finite cumulative factor. Volume is observed raw volume.
Source bytes are verified against the existing transfer receipt. This
does not independently establish vendor values or membership accuracy.

Paper date ranges are kept: train Jan4 2021-Sep28 2023, validation Oct9-
Dec29 2023, test Jan2-Dec31 2024. The 16-step lookback now spans daily
sessions. Normalization fits only training-date historical constituents,
per factor, ignores nonfinite values, uses population mean/std, and clips
to [-5,5]. It preserves NaNs instead of imputing them. Eligible stocks
require finite 16x158 windows and positive current price/factor/volume.
Membership is checked at decision t. Past values can predate membership.
No future outcome or final-period surviving universe determines eligibility.

To give forecasts an explicit execution delay, the daily target is adjusted
close[t+2]/adjusted close[t+1]-1. This differs from the paper's immediate
15-minute forward raw return. Train/validation labels are purged when
their exit lies beyond the split. Test contains all 242 decisions and a
two-session 2025 label-completion tail. Historical context may precede a
split; later outcomes never enter training labels or normalization.

Portfolio interpretation
------------------------
The paper says 'naive long-short 50', without specifying leg leverage,
neutrality, rebalancing/turnover definition, borrow restrictions, or IR
benchmark. Our daily experiment targets +0.5 NAV on 50 highest-scored
stocks and -0.5 NAV on 50 lowest. Ties resolve by sorted instrument ID.
Rebalance at t+1 close using forecasts formed at t close; marked returns
run to t+2 close. This idealized timing is not a verified fill model.

Trading status is observed at execution t+1, not used to filter forecasts
at decision t. Missing/nonpositive entry volume or missing adjusted close
blocks new trades and freezes existing untradable positions. Stocks are
selected from the remaining forecasts without using their future returns.
Existing frozen weights consume that leg's half-NAV budget. Frozen dollar
positions are preserved across rebalance fees, so small exposure deviations
can occur; actual maximum net/gross exposures are reported. Last-observed
adjusted closes mark suspended positions; raw missing labels stay missing
in prediction scoring. Terminal NAV includes an estimated liquidation
fee on all remaining marked positions, including any untradable ones.

The stated paper fee is interpreted as 10bp on liquidated notional: long
sales and short covers pay; opening/increasing exposure is free. Fees use
drifted holdings and post-fee target NAV, not an unconditional daily tax.
Final liquidation is included. Borrow fees, financing, spread, slippage,
price-limit fills, A-share short eligibility, and T+1 trading constraints
are not modeled. Reported P&L is a diagnostic under these assumptions,
not evidence of a directly executable A-share strategy.
The source's corporate-action factors and adjusted price returns are not
a separately vendor-verified cash-dividend accounting dataset.

IC/RankIC average daily cross-sectional correlations; ICIR/RankICIR divide
by population daily standard deviation without annualization. Portfolio
Sharpe uses mean/std(ddof=1)*sqrt(252), risk-free return zero. Annualized
return is compounded NAV^(252/days)-1; drawdown includes initial NAV=1.
Both gross and net figures are reported. Paper IR is left undefined because
its benchmark is unspecified; no Sharpe proxy is relabeled as IR.
Date-block confidence intervals describe sampling uncertainty within this
single test year; three seeds are not independent market observations.

Readiness for exact replication
------------------------------
The model accepts actual 400-factor synchronized tensors, including the
400-dimensional shape in tests. No artificial minute bars or synthetic
private factors are used as empirical evidence. An exact Table3 replication
requires the unavailable author inputs and missing protocol details.
Published reference figures are stored separately and labeled 15-minute;
no numerical daily-vs-intraday difference is presented as replication error.

sources/pilot/ contains an interrupted training-only performance pilot of
the loop loss implementation. No pilot test forecasts were generated or
used. The final suite uses the vectorized equivalent verified in tests.
